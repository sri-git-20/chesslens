# ChessLens privacy policy

_Last updated: 13 November 2026_

ChessLens does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data.

## What stays on your phone

- Photos, screenshots and PDF pages you scan are processed on your phone.
- Recognized positions, collections, puzzle progress and settings are saved
  only in the app's storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

## Permissions

- **Camera**: used only when you tap Camera to photograph a diagram.
- **Internet**: used only if you choose to download the full-strength engine
  network, or tap a link that opens a website. Scanning, analysis, play and
  solving work offline.

The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## Bug reports

If something goes wrong, ChessLens can prepare an email with technical details
(app version, phone model, operating system version and an error log). Nothing
is sent unless you review the email in your own mail app and tap Send. For
recognition problems you can choose whether to attach the image.

Reports go to proxify1@gmail.com and are used only to fix problems.

## Children

ChessLens collects no data from anyone, including children.
